How to read the grids: Each recipe shows the 3×3 crafting table exactly as you place items. Empty cells (grey) are left blank. Place the shown items in the exact positions, and the result appears in the output slot. Items in bold colored cells are what you put in — the color tells you the material type. For any tier tool (wood/stone/iron/gold/diamond), just swap the material shown.

10. Smelting — Furnace Recipes

How the furnace works: Place the ingredient in the top slot, fuel (coal, charcoal, wood, lava bucket, etc.) in the bottom slot, and wait. Each operation takes about 10 seconds. A single piece of coal smelts 8 items. Multiple furnaces can run at once for faster processing.

12. Nether Portal

Building a Nether Portal

Requires: 10 to 14 Obsidian + Flint and Steel

Obsidian
Obsidian
Obsidian
Obsidian
Obsidian
Obsidian
Obsidian
Obsidian
Obsidian
Obsidian

How to get obsidian: Pour a water bucket onto lava source blocks (not flowing lava). Mine with a diamond pickaxe. You need at least 10 obsidian for a minimum portal.

Shape: Minimum 4 blocks wide × 5 blocks tall (inside dimensions). Max 23×23. Light the inside bottom block with Flint and Steel.

Tip: You can save obsidian by leaving the corners out (needs only 10 instead of 14). The corners of the frame don’t need to be obsidian.

Nether Portal - Quick Facts:
• Must be a vertical rectangle, at least 4×5 (inside)
• Corners are optional — save 4 obsidian
• Light with Flint and Steel on the bottom block inside
• You can place water inside to stop pigmen from coming through (Java edition)
• Coordinates in Nether correspond ~1:8 to Overworld
• Destroy the portal by breaking any obsidian frame block or placing water on the activated portal
